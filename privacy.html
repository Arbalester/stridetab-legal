Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

StrideTab is a Chrome new-tab extension designed to work locally first. This policy explains what the extension stores, what it sends when optional services are enabled, and how you can contact the project.

In shortThe core workspace is stored in your browser and does not require an account. Optional cloud features, feedback and product activity use Supabase only when configured for the release.

1. Information stored on your device

StrideTab stores workspace settings, quick links, tasks, blocked domains, widget layouts, Pomodoro state, reminders and screen settings in Chrome local storage. Custom background images are kept locally in the browser. Diagnostic logs, timer runtime data and reminder delivery receipts remain on the device.

2. Optional cloud storage

If a release is configured with Supabase and you enable an account, selected durable workspace data may be mirrored to owner-bound Supabase rows. Supabase Row Level Security restricts these rows to the account owner. Device-only diagnostics, active runtimes, reminder receipts, credentials and provider tokens are excluded.

3. Feedback

The in-app feedback form can send the name, email address, message, language and extension version to the configured Supabase project. Feedback messages and support replies are stored in a support thread. The form is not sent when Supabase is unavailable.

4. Product activity and diagnostics

When cloud services are enabled, StrideTab may record limited activity events such as an app opening or a feature being used. Events contain an allow-listed action, a coarse screen name and small technical flags. They do not contain task text, notes, saved URLs, browsing history, storage values, message contents or credentials. Error diagnostics are kept locally and are not uploaded automatically.

5. Network services

Optional widgets may contact third-party services to provide their feature: Google Search suggestions, Open-Meteo weather/geocoding, ipwho.is approximate location, RSS feeds, CoinGecko or Yahoo Finance quotes, and favicon resolvers. The extension sends only the information needed for the selected request. Local history suggestions are read only after you invoke that feature and are not uploaded to StrideTab.

6. Sharing and selling

StrideTab does not sell personal data or use it for advertising or credit decisions. Data is shared with Supabase and the selected widget providers only when needed to provide the enabled feature. Payment processing, if enabled in a future release, will be handled by the disclosed payment provider rather than by the extension.

7. Your choices

You can use the core extension without an account, disable optional widgets, clear local extension data through Chrome, sign out of an account, and delete a registered account through the extension where that feature is available. For a privacy question or deletion request, use the Feedback link in StrideTab Settings and include an email address if you need a reply.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the extension's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes should also be disclosed in the extension and its store listing.
