Why StrideTab

Everything useful.
Nothing shouting.

StrideTab gives your new tab a job: help you see what matters, take the next step and get on with your day.

01

Start with the essentials

Time, search, tasks, weather, quick links and a timer are ready on the first screen. No setup ritual required.

02

Shape the space around you

Move widgets, resize the layout and create separate screens for home, work or study. The canvas stays yours.

03

Protect your attention

Focus mode and Pomodoro help you make room for deep work without making the browser feel hostile.
